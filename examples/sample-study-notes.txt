Water cycle
Evaporation changes liquid water into water vapour. Condensation changes water vapour into liquid droplets. Clouds contain tiny water droplets or ice crystals. Precipitation is water falling from clouds as rain, snow or hail. Collection occurs when water gathers in rivers, lakes and oceans. The Sun supplies energy that drives evaporation in the water cycle.

Computer memory
The CPU executes program instructions. RAM stores data that a running program is using. RAM is volatile, so its contents are normally lost when power is removed. An SSD stores files even when power is off. A keyboard is an input device. A monitor is an output device. A byte contains eight bits.
